## 1. Executive Introduction & System Architecture
Welcome to the **Dog Breed Life Expectancy Platform (DogHealthOS™)**, a premium comprehensive digital pet care suite. Unlike standard, flat-rate dog aging formulas (such as the outdated "one human year equals seven dog years" myth), this platform utilizes state-of-the-art size-adjusted regression models calibrated against contemporary veterinary data (derived from the Royal Veterinary College VetCompass™, AVMA, AAHA, and university cohort genetic studies), combined with an AI-powered Pro Care Suite (Diagnostics, Nutri Chef, Live Voice, Portrait Studio, and Multi-Specialty Chatbots).

This application empowers you to understand your canine companion's developmental stage, check historical biological milestones, anticipate health flags inherent to their specific breed genetics, adapt feeding or wellness schedules to maximize active, happy longevity, and manage kennel cohorts or clinical rosters.

## 2. Interactive App Interface Tour & Feature Walkthroughs

Our single-screen bento interface has been crafted to avoid page-loading fatigue. Here is a guided walk-through of the operational zones:

### 🌟 Phase A: Selecting the Breed & Current Age
* **Live Autocomplete Search Filter:** Begin typing your dog's breed name in the input box. The system supports full-text lookup across 62 core breeds and 119 total registry variants. For example, typing "Yorkie" or "Retriever" immediately screens matching candidates.
* **Size Class Pill Filter:** If you are unsure of the breed name, you can narrow down selection matrices by clicking the size pills: **Toy**, **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, or **Giant**.
* **Visual Hover Preview Cards:** Hovering over any breed list item immediately projects an informational tooltip outlining typical lifespan parameters, standard adult weights, origin countries, and local high-res portrait fallbacks.
* **Stepper Age Picker:** Use the bold Brutalist `+` and `-` buttons to set your companion's age down to the half-year (0.5), or key in values directly. Alternatively, quick-tap our shortcut tags (e.g., *Puppy 6m*, *Veteran 10y*) for immediate adjustment.

### 🌟 Phase B: The Proportional Development Timeline
Once you hit **"Calculate Live Lifespan Metrics"**, the screen automatically centers on your customized results:
* **Interactive Timeline Marker:** A sliding pin drops dynamically onto a color-coded stage track. This track is proportionately scaled to your breed's maximum historical lifespan. You are shown precisely where your dog sits within their natural lifespan arc.
* **Segment Legend:** We demarcate the 5 universal development markers:
  1. 🔴 **Puppyhood:** Early physical development and high sensory learning.
  2. 🟡 **Young Adult:** Ultimate structural prime, athletic peak, training solidification.
  3. 🟢 **Adult:** Behavioral steadiness, deeply forged emotional bounds.
  4. 🟤 **Senior:** Gradual metabolism cooling, silvering hair patterns.
  5. 🟣 **Golden Years:** Delicate care requirements, immense emotional warmth.

### 🌟 Phase C: Deep-Dive Statistics (The Bento Result Blocks)
* **Expected Lifespan Range:** Showcases the minimum and maximum lifespan bounds, accompanied by a dynamic progress bar charting their average breed expectancy.
* **Human Chronology Mapping:** Translates chronological dog years into a human age equivalent adjusted dynamically for their skeletal size category.
* **Milestone Progress Tracker:** Visualizes completed and upcoming chronological landmarks, celebrating key birthdays.
* **Health Watch Panel:** Surfaces hereditary, breed-specific medical risk indicators. Having early awareness of these markers enables focused discussions with your veterinarian during periodic checks.
* **Brachycephalic Airway (BOAS) Alert Banner:** Flat-faced breeds (French Bulldogs, Pugs, English Bulldogs) trigger dedicated clinical warnings regarding heat intolerance, stenotic nares, and elongated soft palates.

## 4. Professional Breeder Pro Suite (Enterprise 5-Module Kernel)

For professional kennels and preservation breeding programs, the **Breeder Pro Suite** (accessible via the top navigation bar, Pricing Modal, or Admin Console) provides a dedicated 5-module operating kernel:

   - Dam and Sire registry with multi-litter cohort tracking.
   - Individual puppy telemetry tracking collar colors, birth weights, and daily growth velocities (grams or ounces).
   - ISO 11784/11785 compliant microchip assignment and health verification status.

   - Instant generation of official client handoff passports stamped with kennel credentials.
   - Includes vaccination schedules (DA2PP, Parvovirus, Rabies), deworming records, and veterinary sign-off block.

3. **Module 03: OFA / AKC Genetic Clearance Matrix:**
   - Interactive Mendelian Punnett simulation for prospective dam x sire pairings.
   - Computes Clear ($N/N$), Carrier ($N/A$), and Affected ($A/A$) distributions for PRA, DM, vWD, MDR1, and EIC.
   - Integrated OFA/CHIC health screening guidance badges.

   - Computes dam Resting Energy Requirements ($RER = 70 \times \text{weight}_{kg}^{0.75}$) and peak lactation multipliers ($3.5 \times RER$) based on litter size.
   - Stage-by-stage 4-phase weaning porridge progression timeline (Weeks 3 to 8).
5. **Module 05: Adopter Pipeline CRM & Buyer Handoff:**
   - Visual Kanban pipeline tracking prospective buyers from screening to deposit and placement.

## 6. Security, Storage & Rate-Limit Resilience Protocols

### 🔒 Client-Side AES-256 Medical Vault Encryption
All sensitive pet medical records, vaccination dates, and clinical notes stored in the Medical Vault are encrypted client-side using **AES-256-GCM** prior to persistence. Secret keys are held exclusively in user session storage and never transmitted in plaintext.

### 🛡️ Gemini API Rate-Limit Resilience (HTTP 429 Graceful Fallback)
When high server traffic causes the Gemini API to return a `429 Too Many Requests` or `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` status, the platform automatically intercepts the error and serves verified veterinary-certified fallback responses. The user experience remains seamless without breaking UI components or throwing unhandled errors.

### 🖼️ 100% Local Image Fallback Architecture
All 62 primary canine breeds are mapped to optimized local pencil-sketch/photographic assets inside `/public/dog-images/`. If external AI image generation is unavailable or rate-limited, the application renders these local assets with zero visual latency.
