## 2. Elite Pro Breeder Membership: 5-Phase Onboarding Roadmap

### 📋 Phase B-1: Account Setup & Credential Verification (Days 1–2)
* **Goal:** Establish verified kennel identity, ensure adherence to ethical breeding standards, and customize institutional branding.
1. **License & Registry Validation:**
   - Submit kennel registration certificates (AKC, UKC, FCI, or national breed club).
   - Submit municipal breeding permits and USDA Class A/B inspection reports (where applicable).
   - Sign the *DogHealthOS Ethical Preservation Breeding Oath* (commitment to zero puppy-mill operations and strict genetic screening).
2. **Kennel Profile Configuration:**
   - Upload high-resolution kennel logo, banner, and verified physical location.
   - Configure contact information, public bio, and active breed specializations.
   - Set up default measurement units (Grams/Ounces for neonates, Pounds/Kilograms for adults).

### 📋 Phase B-2: Breeding Stock & Genetic Clearance Upload (Days 3–5)
* **Goal:** Populate active sires and dams into the genetic database and input certified laboratory results.
1. **Breeding Stock Registration:**
   - Register all active breeding dams and sires with call names, registered names, microchip IDs, and birthdates.
   - Attach multi-generation pedigrees and lineage coefficient of inbreeding (COI) benchmarks.
2. **Genetic Health Clearance Verification:**
   - Upload official OFA certificates (Hips, Elbows, Eyes, Cardiac, Patellas).
   - Upload PennHIP distraction index radiograph certificates.
   - Input certified DNA panel results (Embark, Wisdom Panel, Paw Print Genetics, or GenSol):
     * Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA-prcd, PRA1, PRA2)
     * Degenerative Myelopathy (DM - SOD1)
     * Von Willebrand Disease (vWD Type I & II)
     * Multidrug Resistance Mutation (MDR1)
     * Exercise-Induced Collapse (EIC)
     * Brachycephalic Airway Obstruction Syndrome (BOAS) functional respiratory grading.

### 📋 Phase B-3: Active Litter Initialization & Neonatal Telemetry (Days 6–8)
* **Goal:** Master the Litter Telemetry engine to log whelping, daily growth velocities, and collar color assignments.
1. **Litter Cohort Creation:**
   - Pair registered dam and sire in the system.
   - Set whelping date, delivery method (natural vs. cesarean), and total puppy count (males/females).
2. **Puppy Identification & Weight Tracking:**
   - Assign standardized collar colors and collar hex codes to each neonate.
   - Log initial birth weights (in grams or ounces).
   - Establish daily weigh-in cadence:
     * *Weeks 1–2:* Weigh every 24 hours to verify minimum 5–10% daily weight velocity gain.
     * *Weeks 3–4:* Weigh every 48 hours to track weaning transition inflection.
     * *Weeks 5–8:* Weigh weekly against breed-standard WALTHAM/AKC growth curves.
3. **Medical Intervention Logs:**
   - Log deworming schedules (Weeks 2, 4, 6, 8 with Pyrantel Pamoate or Fenbendazole).
   - Record first-round DA2PP/DHPP core vaccination dates and manufacturer lot numbers.
   - Assign 15-digit ISO 11784/11785 compliant microchip numbers.

### 📋 Phase B-4: Digital Health Passport Generation & Adopter CRM (Days 9–11)
* **Goal:** Automate buyer communication and export client-facing veterinary longevity passports.
1. **Adopter Pipeline Configuration:**
   - Configure buyer pipeline stages: *Inquiry $\to$ Application Approved $\to$ Deposit Received $\to$ Puppy Matched $\to$ Contract Executed $\to$ Placed*.
   - Input adopter details, contact records, and special dietary/lifestyle requirements.
2. **Branded Veterinary Health Passport Export:**
   - Generate official printable vector PDF passports for each puppy.
   - Verify inclusion of kennel credentials, microchip barcode, growth charts, vaccine stamps, and 30-day feeding transition instructions.
   - Generate the unique puppy QR Emergency Vault code for new owners.

### 📋 Phase B-5: Genetic Simulation & Weaning Math Training (Days 12–14)
* **Goal:** Enable predictive mating tools and lactation nutrition formulas.
1. **Punnett Matrix Breeding Simulations:**
   - Run prospective mating simulations between kennel stock or outside studs to predict allele distribution (Clear / Carrier / Affected).
2. **NRC 2006 Lactation & Weaning Caloric Formulation:**
   - Compute dam's peak lactation caloric needs ($3.5 \times RER$) based on litter size.
   - Review 4-stage weaning porridge progression timeline (solid food introduction at Week 3.5 through total weaning at Week 7.5).

## 3. Veterinary Clinical Suite: 5-Phase Onboarding Roadmap

### 📋 Phase V-1: Practice Licensing & Infrastructure Audit (Days 1–3)
* **Goal:** Verify clinical credentials and establish institutional multi-user security protocols.
1. **Medical License Validation:**
   - Verify medical director's state DVM / VMD veterinary license and DEA registration.
   - Validate American Animal Hospital Association (AAHA) or state veterinary medical association accreditation.
2. **Multi-Staff Credentialing:**
   - Provision up to 10 staff member accounts (Associate Veterinarians, Licensed Vet Technicians, Practice Managers).
   - Set role-based permissions (Admins, Clinicians, Front Desk Staff).

### 📋 Phase V-2: Practice Management System (PIMS/EHR) Integration (Days 4–7)
* **Goal:** Connect clinic software or configure streamlined patient import workflows.
1. **PIMS Integration Protocols:**
   - Configure integration with clinic management platforms (Idexx Cornerstone, Covetrus Avimark, ezyVet, or Hippo Manager).
   - Set up automated patient demographic sync (Breed, Age, Sex, Weight, Neuter status).
2. **Clinic White-Label Customization:**
   - Upload clinic logo, emergency contact phone, after-hours hospital location, and online booking URL.
   - Configure clinic headers and footers across all exported patient medical passports, nutritional plans, and longevity reports.

### 📋 Phase V-3: Clinical NutriChef & Prescriptive Caloric Profiles (Days 8–10)
* **Goal:** Configure clinical dietary formulation modules for acute and chronic conditions.
1. **Caloric Mathematics Setup:**
   - Train veterinary technicians on the Resting Energy Requirement equation ($RER = 70 \times \text{weight}_{kg}^{0.75}$).
   - Configure disease-specific Maintenance Energy Requirement (MER) multipliers:
     * Weight Loss / Obesity Management ($0.8 - 1.0 \times RER$)
     * Geriatric / Sarcopenia Prevention ($1.1 - 1.2 \times RER$)
     * Active Canine Athletic Performance ($2.0 - 3.0 \times RER$)
     * Post-Surgical Recovery & Wound Healing ($1.3 - 1.5 \times RER$)
2. **Prescriptive Diet Matching:**
   - Map partner therapeutic diets (Hill's Prescription Diet, Royal Canin Veterinary Diet, Purina Pro Plan Veterinary Diets) into client handoff templates.

### 📋 Phase V-4: Multi-Patient Biometric Telemetry & Remote Monitoring (Days 11–13)
* **Goal:** Deploy remote weight logging and condition monitoring for high-risk patients.
1. **Patient Roster Enrollment:**
   - Enroll geriatric patients, post-orthopedic surgery cases, and chronic BOAS/cardiac management patients.
   - Configure target weight velocity curves and automatic threshold alerts (e.g., flag any patient losing $>2\%$ body weight in 7 days).
2. **Digital Health Vault QR Pass Handout:**
   - Issue client emergency QR badges during discharge, enabling owners to carry encrypted AES-256 records on their mobile phones.

### 📋 Phase V-5: Staff Clinical Triage & AI Care Center Certification (Day 14)
* **Goal:** Train veterinary nurses and front-desk triage staff on Dr. Barnes AI Clinical Center protocols.
1. **Clinical AI Triage Protocol:**
   - Educate staff on the Evidence-Based Citation framework (AVMA, AAHA, AAFCO, NRC).
   - Establish guidelines on utilizing AI diagnostic differential generation as a clinical co-pilot during preliminary exams.
2. **Formal Certification & Sign-Off:**
   - Conduct simulated patient intake exam.
   - Sign institutional operational clearance and receive official *DogHealthOS Certified Clinical Partner* badge.
