### 4. FRENCH BULLDOG CLINICAL CARE PROTOCOL & HYGIENE
*(Customized by Elysian Frenchies & DogHealthOS Clinical Advisory Board)*

1. **Brachycephalic Airway & Thermal Safety**:
   - French Bulldogs cannot dissipate heat efficiently. **Never leave Winston in temperatures above 78°F (25.5°C)** without air conditioning.
   - **Harness Only**: Always use a padded chest harness (never attach a leash to a neck collar) to prevent tracheal collapse or respiratory pressure.
2. **Skin Fold & Tail Pocket Maintenance**:
   - Clean facial wrinkles and tail pocket 3x weekly using chlorhexidine/ketoconazole wipes. Keep folds thoroughly dry to avoid pyoderma.
3. **Spinal Protection (IVDD Prevention)**:
   - Frenchies are chondrodysplastic. Do not allow Winston to jump on or off furniture or high beds. Provide ramps or pet stairs.
