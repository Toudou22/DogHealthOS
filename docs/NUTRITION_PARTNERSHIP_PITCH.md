## 3. Caloric & Portion Matching Engineering

DogHealthOS removes guessing games for pet owners by calculating exact meal portioning based on **NRC (National Research Council) Resting Energy Requirement (RER)** equations:

$$\text{RER (kcal/day)} = 70 \times (\text{Weight in kg})^{0.75}$$
$$\text{MER (Maintenance Energy Requirement)} = \text{RER} \times \text{Life Stage Factor}$$

When a user views your product recommendation:
1. The app automatically calculates how many grams, cups, or fresh packs of your specific formula equal [Pet Name]'s exact MER target.
2. The user sees: *"Feed [Pet Name] exactly 1.5 cups / 1.2 packs per day of [Your Brand Formula] for optimal weight maintenance."*
3. Conversion spikes because the pet owner feels complete clinical confidence in the feeding plan.
